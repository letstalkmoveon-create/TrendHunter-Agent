Self-Help & Non-Fiction Market Gap Report

2026-08-21 · Topics scanned: growth mindset habits
The general growth-mindset/habits book market is heavily saturated (the same handful of titles resurface across nearly every bookstore searched), but the highest-engagement content in this dataset by a wide margin is women-focused 'queen mindset' YouTube content — and no dedicated book in the search results matches that positioning. Short, countdown-style habit content also dramatically outperforms research-citation-style framing, suggesting audience fatigue with the dense, academic tone most existing books use.

5 High-Demand Trends

1. Female-specific 'queen mindset' content is winning attention that no matching book exists to capture

The top two YouTube results by engagement (74,764 and a Simon-Sinek-style 'Queen Mindset' talk) are both framed specifically for women's self-respect, confidence, and boundary-setting — not generic growth mindset. None of the 36 bookstore results returned a book with that specific positioning; the closest match ('Goal Setting for Women') is a different angle entirely.
